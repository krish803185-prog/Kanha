KANHA // DEPTH OPTIC · Z-REACH

RELAY MODEL -- FPS
REACH (Z-DEPTH)—
Place this phone / second laptop 45°–90° to your side, at shoulder height, so it sees your arm moving toward the screen.
1. Start the optic. 2. Hold your hand at your chest and press Calibrate: hand at chest. 3. Extend your arm toward the screen and press Calibrate: arm extended.
Only landmark JSON is sent to the relay (no video). Phones require HTTPS — start the OS with npm run start:lan and accept the local certificate.
